# Code of Conduct

This project serves churches and the people in them, including children and people in need. We expect everyone who takes part to act accordingly.

## Our standards
- Be respectful, patient and kind. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Welcome contributors of every background, denomination, experience level and language.
- Keep discussions constructive and on topic. No harassment, insults, discrimination, or unwelcome attention.
- Treat all personal data as sensitive. Never post real names, photos or details of people served by a church in issues, pull requests or discussions.
- Respect the faith context of the project without using it to pressure or exclude others.

## Child protection
Do not contact minors through project channels. Any concern about the safety of a child should be reported to the maintainers privately.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and ban participants who violate these standards. To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub (repository owner profile) or the contact listed in SECURITY.md.
